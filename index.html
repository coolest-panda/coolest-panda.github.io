Privacy & Data Governance

Bhumi Calculator

Official Privacy Policy & Data Transparency Statement

✓ 100% Offline & Zero Data Collection

Bhumi Calculator is built from the ground up to operate completely offline on your device. We do not collect, monitor, store, share, or transmit any personal data, device identifiers, or land measurements.

1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy explains the privacy practices for the Bhumi Calculator mobile application (Application ID: com.bhumicalc.app).

Bhumi Calculator is an Indian regional land-area measurement and conversion calculator designed to convert between traditional land revenue units (Bigha, Biswa, Kanal, Marla, Kattha, Dhur, Guntha, Ground, Cent, Acre, Hectare, Square Feet, and Square Meters) across regional revenue standards.

Your privacy is our core priority. The application is architected to perform all computations locally on your device without communicating with external servers.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

Bhumi Calculator does not collect any user data. Specifically:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account details.
  • No Device Information: We do not collect IMEI, Android ID, IP addresses, MAC addresses, or hardware fingerprints.
  • No Location Data: The application does not request GPS, coarse location, or network-based location permissions.
  • No Sensor or Camera Access: The app requires no access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or storage.
  • No Financial Data: The app does not process payments, bank details, or transactions.

3. Android Permissions Transparency

Bhumi Calculator requests zero runtime permissions in its AndroidManifest.xml:

  • No INTERNET permission (The app cannot communicate over the network).
  • No ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permission.
  • No READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE permissions.
  • No READ_MEDIA_* permissions.

4. Local Device Storage (UI Preferences Only)

The application only utilizes Android's local SharedPreferences on your device to remember user interface preferences, such as:

  • Keypad Haptic Feedback: Whether tactile vibration on keypress is enabled or disabled.
  • Active Regional Standard: The currently selected state conversion standard (e.g. Standard UP, Punjab/Haryana, MP, etc.).

These preferences remain strictly on your device and are never backed up, uploaded, or transmitted anywhere.

5. Non-Affiliation with Government Entities

Bhumi Calculator is an independent mathematical utility developed for educational and estimation purposes. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or representing any government entity or state revenue department (such as Bhulekh, Bhoomi, or any state land records portal).

All calculations are mathematical estimates. For official property boundary demarcation, registration, or legal disputes, users should always consult certified government land records (Khasra, Khatauni, RoR) and licensed revenue surveyors.

6. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Bhumi Calculator contains zero third-party trackers, zero advertising SDKs, and zero telemetry services. We do not integrate with Google AdMob, Unity, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or any third-party behavioral profiling networks.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Because Bhumi Calculator does not collect any personal data from any user, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). The application is safe for users of all ages.
